# Features
The input to the model is a tensor representing the position of the robot's gripper and the door at the current and previous time step.

Features: B x 39 where B is the batch size
0-2. The x, y, z coordinates of the gripper at the current time step.
3. A scalar value that represents how open/close the gripper is at the current time step.
4-6. The x, y, z coordinates of the door.
7-10. The quaternion (x, y, z, w) representing the orientation of the door.
11-17. Not used
18-20. The x, y, z coordinates of the gripper at the previous time step.
21. A scalar value that represents how open/close the gripper was at the previous time step.
22-24. The x, y, z coordinates of the door at the previous time step.
25-28. The quaternion (x, y, z, w) representing the orientation of the door at the previous time step.
29-35. Not used
36-38. The x, y, z coordinates of the target position for the door to be opened to.

# Output Space
A tensor of shape (B, 4) where the first three elements are the x, y, z motion of the gripper relative to its current position, and the last element is the gripper open/close command (1 for open, 0 for close).

# Objective and Reward
The objective is to reach for the handle on the door and pull the door open to a desired angle. The reward is given by the distance to the desired angle, when the gripper first hold onto the handle, and with a large positive reward for successfully opening the door. A small negative reward for each time step taken.

# Additional Notes
* The door revolves around a vertical axis.
* The door handle is horizontal.
* The gripper always points down.
* Name your model "OpenDoorPolicy".
